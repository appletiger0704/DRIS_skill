Recipe lookup workflow for general-meal-planner

Before designing a menu, search trusted cuisine sources for real dish ideas.

Preferred sources:
1. TasteAtlas: traditional dishes and local ingredients.
2. Gastronomixs: ingredient pairing and flavor building blocks.
3. The World's 50 Best Restaurants: modern restaurant trends and cooking techniques.

Workflow:
1. Identify patient limits and nutrition targets.
2. Identify cuisine style requested by the user.
3. Collect candidate dishes from the sources above.
4. Extract only dish structure, flavor direction, ingredient pairing, and cooking technique.
5. Do not copy full recipes or long source text.
6. Adapt each dish for the patient by changing ingredients, seasoning, portion size, and cooking method.
7. Map all ingredients to the DRIS workbook food database.
8. Write the menu into the official DRIS Excel template.
9. Check calories, macronutrients, vitamins, minerals, and disease-specific limits.
10. If results fail, adjust the dish candidates and recalculate.

Output should include a short source inspiration note for each adopted dish.
